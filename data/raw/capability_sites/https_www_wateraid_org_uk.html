Help us change the world through water

Along with decent toilets and good hygiene, clean water is essential for health, dignity and a life full of opportunity.

Donate now

Millions of people are still living without these essentials

696 million

Worldwide, 696 million people – almost 1 in 10 – don’t have clean water close to home.*
Our work on water

1.5 billion

1.5 billion people don't have a decent toilet of their own.*
Discover more about the power of a decent toilet

1.7 billion

1.7 billion people don't have soap and water to wash their hands at home.*

Who we are

We’re an international non-profit organisation with one goal: to change the world through water.

Learn more about who we are

What we do

We work with our partners – from local communities to global allies – to reach everyone with clean water, decent toilets and good hygiene.

Find out what we do

Where your money goes

Your generous support helps entire communities to thrive, thanks to the life-changing power of clean water – not just now, but for generations to come.

Cup of coffee
